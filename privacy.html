Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information DRYPE collects when you shop with us, and how it's used.

1. Information we collect

  • Name, phone number, delivery address, city, state and pincode — collected at checkout to deliver your order.
  • Order details — items purchased, amounts, and order status.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, used only to make the site work correctly.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, ship and track your order.
  • To contact you about your order via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment information

Payments are made directly through your UPI app. DRYPE does not collect or store your UPI PIN, card numbers, or banking credentials at any point.

4. Where your data is stored

Order and product information is stored securely using our hosting provider's data storage service, accessible only to store administrators.

5. Third parties

We share your delivery details with our courier partner (DTDC) solely to fulfil and track your shipment.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us below.
