Shipping & Returns

Last updated: September 2026

Shipping

  • All orders are shipped via DTDC once payment is confirmed.
  • Orders are typically dispatched within 2–4 business days of confirmed payment.
  • You'll receive your DTDC AWB/consignment number so you can track your order on the Track Order page.
  • Delivery timelines depend on your location and DTDC's service in your area, generally 3–7 business days after dispatch.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, use the tracking ID we send you on our Track Order page, or directly on the official DTDC tracking page.

Returns & exchanges

  • If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging.
  • Approved returns/exchanges must be shipped back in original, unused condition with tags attached.
  • Refunds for approved returns are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the returned item.
  • Sizing exchanges are subject to stock availability.

Order issues

Wrong address entered at checkout, missed courier delivery attempts, or refused deliveries may result in delays or additional shipping charges for re-delivery.
